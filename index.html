What is MyFlous

A money tracker that stays out of your business — literally.

MyFlous started from a simple frustration: most finance apps want your bank login, your phone number, and a subscription before they'll show you a single chart. MyFlous doesn't. It's a self-contained ledger that lives on your phone, built for students, employees and anyone who wants a clear answer to "where did my money go this month?" — without handing their financial life to a server somewhere.

Open it, add your accounts, start logging. That's the whole onboarding.
